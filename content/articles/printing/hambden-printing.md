## The Home Office Printer Trap

Home printers are designed to sell ink. The cartridge costs $40. It prints 200 pages. The printer costs $60. It prints 200 pages and then demands another $40 cartridge. The margins work for the manufacturer. They do not work for you when the cartridge runs dry at 10:32 on the morning of a bid deadline.

You need eighteen copies of a six-page bid for a Hambden Township property. You need them stapled. You need them now.

The home printer is not going to deliver.
