## Your Kirtland Hills Business Card Looks DIY. It's Time for a Rebrand.

You hand the card across the table. The prospect looks at it. The edges are rough. The ink is smudged. The logo looks like clip art.

They do not say anything. They do not have to. The card says enough.

A DIY business card tells a story. The story is not the one you want.

### What a DIY Card Communicates

The person who made that card does not care about details. The person who printed it at home saved $50. The person who designed it had no training.

A client sees all of that in two seconds. They judge the business before you speak.

### The Problem With Templates

Templates work for everyone. That means they work for no one. Your competitor in Concord Township uses the same layout. The realtor in Mentor uses the same font. The plumber in Willoughby uses the same color scheme.

A template is not a brand. It is a placeholder.

### The Conspiracy of the Stock Image

Stock images appear on every template. A handshake. A building. A woman with a headset.

These images have no connection to Kirtland Hills. They do not reflect your business. They do not reflect your clients. They reflect a designer who does not know you.
