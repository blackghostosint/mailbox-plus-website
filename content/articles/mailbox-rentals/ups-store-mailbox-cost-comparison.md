## The Number You Cannot Find Online

You searched "ups private mailbox cost" for a reason: you want a number before you drive anywhere, and the big-brand mailbox pages will not give you one. Every UPS Store is an independently owned franchise, the national site does not post a rate chart, and the Mentor-area store's own mailbox page answers the pricing question with "pricing for mailbox services will be dependent on a number of factors." So you are left calling stores, one at a time, just to learn whether the box fits your budget.

Here is the only number either side will put on paper: Mailbox Plus at $35 and $50 a month. The Mentor UPS Store will not publish one — what you get instead is a fee stack you hear on the phone.

## The Unquoted Quote

Here's the machine: no corporate rate card. Each franchise owner builds a quote from a box rate you only hear in person — then stacks an initial setup charge, a key deposit, a late fee if rent slips, a per-day storage fee if a box sits unclaimed, and an add-a-name charge. One line at a time.

None of it is hidden. All of it is disclosed — in a mailbox service agreement you sign after the conversation. The number you compared in your head ("a mailbox is about twenty bucks a month") is rarely the number on the agreement, because the box rate is only one line of the stack. But the structure means the shopper comparing costs is comparing the one number nobody quotes.

## How Signing Up Works

1. **Bring two IDs.** One photo ID, one more, and we complete USPS Form 1583 with you at the counter — it authorizes the store to receive your mail. Every additional name on the box completes one too.
2. **Pick your tier and pay one month.** $35 or $50, stated before you commit. No appointment needed.
3. **Start using the address same day.** It is a real street address with your box as a PMB designator — carriers deliver to it, and you can put it on business documents, seller profiles, and registrations.
