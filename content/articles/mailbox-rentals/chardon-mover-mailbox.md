Again.

Every move comes with the same ritual. Fill out the USPS change-of-address form. Update your bank. Update your credit cards. Tell Amazon. Tell the DMV. Tell your employer. Tell the insurance company. Miss one and your car registration goes to the old place and you do not know it until the ticket arrives.

Mailbox Plus stops the cycle. One real street address. You move from an apartment on the square to a house on the edge of town — the address stays the same. Your mail follows you. Your packages arrive. All four carriers deliver. You update nothing because nothing changed.
