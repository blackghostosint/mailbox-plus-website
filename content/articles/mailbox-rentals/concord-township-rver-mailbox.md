## What's Really Behind The Thirty-Day Wall

Call it what it is: **the Thirty-Day Wall**.

The default tools are built for people who are moving, not people who are leaving. USPS Hold Mail pauses everything for a minimum of 3 days and a maximum of 30 days — not 3 months, not 6. A temporary change of address covers 15 days up to a year, but it forwards primarily First-Class Mail and periodicals piece by piece, and generally does not forward Marketing Mail or Package Services. The letters trickle through. The packages don't. The catalogs pile up on an empty porch in Ohio while you're parked in Arizona.

And even the mail that forwards lands on a moving target. A forwarding order goes to one address. You have five between October and April. So the real system most travelers run is a patchwork: a neighbor holding the pile, a relative opening things they shouldn't have to, a hold order that lapses mid-trip, a package marked "delivered" to a dark house. None of it is a scam. All of it is a gap — and the gap is thirty days wide.
