Maybe this is the year you leave before the first snow. Point the RV south toward Florida, Arizona, somewhere the ground is not frozen for four months. The plan is solid. The only problem is your address.

You cannot take your Chardon mailbox with you. But you cannot give it up either — your bank, your insurance, your voter registration, your IRS. All of it is tied to that address. Change it and you spend the winter re-routing every piece of mail. Keep it and your mail piles up on a frozen porch or gets forwarded weekly in a fat bundle that is bent, wet, and useless.
