## Your Kirtland Hills Tax Returns Are Sitting in a Box in the Garage.

The box sits on a shelf. It holds tax returns from 2015. It holds bank statements from 2017. It holds credit card bills from 2019. It holds pay stubs from three jobs ago.

The box has no lock. The garage has a standard door. A thief pops the lock in seconds. The box is the first thing they grab.

Your name. Your address. Your Social Security number. Your bank account numbers. Your employer details. All in one box. All in plain text.

That box is a risk.

### What Happens When a Box Goes Missing

A stolen box means more than lost paper. Identity thieves use your tax returns to file a fake return. They use your bank statements to forge checks. They use your pay stubs to apply for credit.

The Federal Trade Commission tracks these cases. The number grows every year. The IRS publishes safeguarding guidelines for a reason.

### The Data You Should Not Keep

The IRS keeps your tax records. Your bank keeps your statements. You do not need to keep them.

Most documents are safe to shred after three years. Tax returns can go after seven. Pay stubs can go after one year. Bank statements can go after one month.

If you have a box from 2015, it is past the mark.
