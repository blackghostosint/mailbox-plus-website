# Your Hambden Passport Expired. We'll Get the Photo Right in 5 Minutes.

![A rejected passport photo with a red stamp reading REJECTED over a shadowed face](articles/document-services/hambden-passport-photos-featured.webp)

Your passport expired in March. The trip to Niagara Falls is in three weeks. You need to renew.

The State Department requires a 2-by-2-inch photo. White background. Even lighting. No shadows. No glasses glare. No smile. Head centered. Specific dimensions from chin to crown. The specifications run a full page on the State Department website.

You stand in your Hambden kitchen. You tape a white sheet to the wall. You take twelve photos with your phone. None of them work. One has a shadow from the ceiling light. One has the top of your head cut off. One has a squint. The background in every shot looks gray in the low light.

You submit the best one to the passport photo app. The app says FAIL — background not white.

## The Rejection Cost

A rejected passport photo means your application gets kicked back. That adds two to four weeks to the processing time. That is two to four weeks closer to the Niagara trip. That is a rescheduled hotel. That is a call to the campground. That is the back-and-forth of approval and rejection that turns a simple renewal into a project.

The photo is the part that should be easy. It is not easy when you are leaning against a sheet in your kitchen.

## Five Minutes, Guaranteed

At **Mailbox Plus**, we take [passport photos](/home-business/notary-services) on a proper setup. Gray backdrop that the system converts to pure white. Studio lighting — even, no shadows, no glare. A camera on a tripod. A measuring mark at the correct distance.

You sit. I frame the shot. Click. The software checks the dimensions. It prints two copies on professional photo paper. You walk out with them.

The whole thing takes five minutes. The photo is guaranteed to meet State Department specifications. If it gets rejected — which it will not — we retake it free.
