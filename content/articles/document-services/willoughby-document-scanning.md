It works like this, in plain daylight. Then the storage burden is entirely yours. The hospital keeps its records digital and redundant. The county keeps its records digital and redundant. Your one copy exists on paper, in cardboard, indexed by your memory. Nobody hands you a backup of your deed at closing. Nobody emails you a second copy of your 2015 return.

Then the home scanner was supposed to be your escape hatch, and that is where the trap really closes. A consumer flatbed is built for twenty pages, not a filing cabinet.
