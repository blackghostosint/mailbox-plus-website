# Your Hambden Tax Returns Are Sitting in a Box in the Garage

![A cardboard storage box in a dusty garage with folders labeled Taxes 2014-2019](articles/document-services/hambden-shredding-featured.webp)

The box is on the garage shelf behind the snow shovels. It holds tax returns from 2014 to 2019. Bank statements. Pay stubs. A credit card application you filled out and did not send. Your Social Security number is in there. Your wife's Social Security number is in there. Your signatures. Your account numbers.

The box has no lock. The garage door does not always close all the way. You noticed that last winter when you found a drift of snow inside by the workbench.

You meant to shred it. You meant to shred it three years ago. The box keeps sitting there.

## The Risk in the Box

A break-in in Hambden is rare. But a break-in does not need to be common to ruin your week. One person pries the side door, walks into the garage, sees the box labeled "Taxes," and carries it out. They have your identity in a cardboard box.

They do not need your wallet. They do not need your computer. They need the box.

The box has everything a data thief needs to file a fraudulent tax return, open a credit card in your name, or drain a bank account. It is all right there in the garage. No encryption. No password. No lock.

## On-Site Shredding, Immediate Destruction

At **Mailbox Plus**, [shredding](/home-business/shredding/) happens on-site. You bring the box in. We empty it into a locked container. A certified operator runs the industrial shredder. The paper becomes confetti. Cross-cut. Unreadable.

You watch if you want. Or you drop the box and pick up a receipt. Either way, the shredding is done while you stand there. No truck takes it somewhere else. No box sits in a warehouse overnight. It is destroyed and gone.

## What to Shred

Tax returns older than seven years. Bank statements past three years. Pay stubs. Utility bills with account numbers. Credit card offers. Old checks. Medical records you no longer need. Any document with a signature, a Social Security number, or a financial account number.

If you look at a piece of paper and think "I should probably shred this," you should shred it.

## The Alternative to the Garage Box

[Document scanning](/home-business/document-scanning/) is the option for papers you need to keep. We scan them into a digital file. You get the PDF. The paper goes into the shredder.

Digital records do not sit in a garage. They do not get carried out in a box. They live on your hard drive and your backup. Accessible. Searchable. Gone from the shelf behind the snow shovels.

The box in the garage is not a filing system. It is a liability. Bring it in. We will destroy it.
