# Three Returns. Three Carriers. One 5-Minute Drive From Kirtland Hills.

The kitchen table is covered in boxes. A pair of boots goes back to Zappos via FedEx. A blender goes back to Amazon via UPS. A sweater goes back to a small shop via USPS.

You check three tracking numbers. You pack three boxes. You print three labels. You need three different drop-off locations.

That is the problem.

## The Return Runaround

The UPS store is on Route 306. The FedEx drop box is near Mentor Avenue. The post office is past downtown. Three stops. Three lines. Thirty minutes minimum.

One store does not take the other carrier's packages. The FedEx box has a pickup time. The post office closes at 5. You waste an afternoon running returns.

Amazon returns use UPS. Zappos returns use FedEx. Etsy sellers use USPS. No single store accepts all three carrier labels. You drive in a triangle around Kirtland Hills.

## The One-Stop Counter

Mailbox Plus sits at 7554 Fredle Drive in Concord Township. One counter accepts all four carriers. UPS. FedEx. USPS. DHL. You hand over every box. We handle the rest.

You bring the Amazon box with a UPS label. You bring the Zappos box with a FedEx label. You bring the Etsy box with a USPS label. We take each one. We give you a receipt. You leave.

Total time. Three minutes.

Check your [USPS tracking](https://tools.usps.com/go/TrackConfirmAction_input) or your [FedEx tracking](https://www.fedex.com/en-us/tracking.html). Everything goes through our counter.

We handle packages every day. The carriers pick up from our location. Your box enters the system from a commercial hub.

## How It Works

You pack your returns at home. You print your labels. You load the boxes in your car. You drive 5 minutes down Route 306 to 7554 Fredle Drive.

You walk to the counter. You place each box on the scale. We scan each label. We sort each package by carrier. We schedule the pickup.

You walk out. Done.

You get a receipt for each package. You have proof of drop-off. You track the return from your phone.
