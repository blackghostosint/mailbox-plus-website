## The Conveyor Belt Gamble

The thing you're up against isn't a careless clerk. It's the system itself.

Every package you hand to a carrier enters an automated sorting network that moves millions of boxes a night. Belts drop packages between chutes. Heavier boxes land on lighter ones. A FRAGILE label is a suggestion no machine can read. And the carriers are honest about it: FedEx's own terms put the responsibility on you — the shipper — to pack the item so it "can be carried safely with ordinary care in handling." Ordinary care, at a national hub, is a drop and a stack. Insurance covers the dollar value you declare. It does not cover the thing itself.

Here's the part most people never think about: that means the entire safety of your heirloom was decided before the label printed. It was decided at the packing step. And a counter that takes your box as-is gives you a label without ever touching the one step that matters.

Concord Township has counters that will ship a box. The variable you actually control isn't the carrier or the counter. It's how well the thing is packed before any belt sees it.
