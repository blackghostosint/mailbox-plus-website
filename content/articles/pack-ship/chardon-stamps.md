You need a stamp. One stamp. Seventy-three cents. The post office near Chardon Square has a line. Three people deep. One window open. The person at the front is shipping a package and asking about insurance. You stand there for ten minutes for a stamp.

Mailbox Plus sells stamps at the counter. Same price as the post office. Forever stamps. Postcard stamps. International stamps. You walk in, ask for a book, pay, and walk out. Thirty seconds.

No line because stamps are not our main business. They are a convenience. You are not waiting behind someone shipping a refrigerator box. You are in and out.

Stamps for the wedding invitations. Stamps for the bills that refuse to accept online payments. Stamps for the birthday card to your niece. Stamps for the property tax payment to Geauga County.

Fifteen minutes from Chardon Square on Auburn Road. Next time you need a stamp, skip the line.
