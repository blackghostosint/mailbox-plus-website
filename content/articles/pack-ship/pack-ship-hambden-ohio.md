# Hambden: Why Drive 25 Miles for a Shipping Counter That Only Shows One Rate?

![A long, quiet rural road winding through Geauga County farmland](articles/pack-ship/pack-ship-hambden-ohio-featured.webp)

You live in Hambden Township. You run a small engine repair shop out of your garage. Or you sell vintage tractor parts on eBay. Or you just need to ship a Christmas present to your daughter in Columbus.

The closest shipping counter is 12 miles away. Through Chardon traffic. Past the square. Into a strip mall where the franchise store sits between a pizza place and a vape shop.

You walk in with your package. The counter person weighs it, types something, and gives you a price. It seems high. But you drove 25 miles round trip to get here. You are not going to drive to another store to compare. So you pay it.

That is the Franchise Markup at work. It is not a sign on the wall. It is the assumption that you will not walk out.

## What You Don't See

Single-carrier franchise stores are not in the business of showing you options. Their screen only knows how to quote their parent company's rates. If FedEx is 20% cheaper for your box going to Cleveland, you will never know. If USPS is half the price for that heavy envelope to Texas, the screen will not show it.

The system works because it trusts geography. You drove far enough that you will not leave. You will grumble and pay.

But you should not have to grumble. You should not have to pay more than the best available rate just because the counter you drove to only carries one flag.
