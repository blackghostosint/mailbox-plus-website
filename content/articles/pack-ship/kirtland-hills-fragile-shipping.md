# That Family Heirloom From Kirtland Hills Needs to Cross the Country Safely.

The box sits on your kitchen counter. Your grandmother's china set is wrapped in newspaper you saved from last Sunday. You tape the seams. You write the address. You hope.

Hope is not a packing strategy.

One bad drop in a sorting center and your irreplaceable item arrives in pieces. Insurance reimburses the receipt, not the memory. That gravy boat your grandmother carried from Ohio in 1952? The receipt says $12. The memory says everything.

The conveyor belt does not care about memories.

## What Goes Wrong Inside a Sorting Center

Packages fly down metal ramps. They collide. They stack under sixty pounds of weight. Cardboard buckles. Tape fails. Newspaper compresses to nothing.

Your china becomes shards before the truck leaves Concord Township.

Sorting centers move ten thousand packages an hour. Your heirloom is one object on a high-speed belt. The belt always wins.

A vase from a Kirtland Hills estate sale faces the same risk. A signed first edition from a Mentor Avenue bookshop faces the same risk. A painting from a Route 306 gallery faces the same risk.

Newspaper cannot stop a forty-pound box landing on top of it.

## Why Professional Packing Changes the Outcome

Mailbox Plus uses double-wall boxes. We use polyfoam inserts. We use air pillows that absorb shock. Each item gets its own wrap.

We do not use newspaper.

Our team packs every fragile item like it belongs to their own grandmother. We measure the gap. We fill the gap. We test the seal.

The box arrives because the box was built for the trip.

Fragile stickers help. They remind handlers to be careful. But the real protection comes from the packing inside the box. We build that protection first.

## Insurance That Means Something

You can insure a package at the counter. But insurance covers the dollar value. It does not unfracture the porcelain.

A full dinner set insured for $500 pays $500 if it breaks. You still have no dinner set. You still have no gravy boat. You still have no memory.

Proper packing makes insurance claims irrelevant. The item arrives whole. No forms. No photos. No arguments.
