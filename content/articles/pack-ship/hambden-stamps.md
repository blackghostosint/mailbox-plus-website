# You Live in Hambden. You Need a Stamp. The Post Office Line Is 20 Minutes.

![A worn linoleum floor and a rope queue at a rural post office counter](articles/pack-ship/hambden-stamps-featured.webp)

The Hambden post office has one window. The clerk handles passports, package drop-offs, money orders, and stamp sales from the same spot. A woman ahead of you in line is disputing a missing package. She has documentation. She has receipts. She is not giving up.

You need one stamp.

It is 2:30 on a Tuesday. You have twenty minutes before you need to pick up your kid at the bus stop. The line has not moved in five minutes.

## The Twenty-Minute Stamp

The post office in Hambden is a good post office. The people who work there are friendly. They handle a lot of volume through one window. That window does everything.

When you stand in that line for a single $0.73 stamp, you are not paying seventy-three cents. You are paying twenty minutes of your afternoon.

The math is worse in December. Worse on tax day. Worse on the last day to renew a passport. The line snakes past the PO box wall, past the outgoing mail slot, past the door. You stand there holding a single envelope and a five-dollar bill.

## The Counter Alternative

At **Mailbox Plus** on Fredle Drive, stamps are at the front counter. You walk in. You ask. We hand them to you.

Same USPS first-class forever stamp. Same price — whatever the current first-class rate is. No line. No number ticket. No waiting while someone disputes a package from three weeks ago.

You hand us a five. We hand you stamps. You walk out. It takes forty-five seconds.

## What Else the Counter Does

The [pack-and-ship counter](/pack-ship/) also takes packages. If the envelope you are stamping is headed somewhere via [FedEx or UPS](/pack-ship/postage-stamps/), we print the label right there. No extra stop. No second line.

You can mail a letter and ship a box in the same visit. The counter handles both.

## The Real Cost of a Stamp

A roll of twenty stamps at the post office costs $14.60. You wait twenty minutes for the privilege of buying it. A book of stamps at Mailbox Plus costs the same $14.60. You wait zero minutes.

The time is the difference. Twenty minutes of standing on worn linoleum versus forty-five seconds at a counter. Same stamp. Same price. Different afternoon.

The Hambden post office window keeps doing what it does. But when you only need stamps, you do not have to stand in that line. The counter at Mailbox Plus is open.
