# Three Returns. Three Carriers. One 25-Mile Drive From Hambden.

![Three packages with different carrier labels stacked on a shipping counter](articles/pack-ship/hambden-returns-r1-featured.webp)

You ordered a blender from Amazon. It arrived with a cracked pitcher. The return label says UPS.

You ordered hiking boots from Zappos. They are half a size too small. The return label says FedEx.

You bought a custom birdhouse on Etsy. It looks nothing like the photo. The return label says USPS.

Three returns. Three carriers. Three different drop-off locations.

You live in Hambden.

## The Math

The UPS Store in Mentor is 12 miles. The FedEx drop box near the Chardon square is 8 miles. The post office on Route 6 is 6 miles. That is 26 miles if you hit all three in one loop. That is an hour of driving. That is a gallon of gas. That is a Tuesday evening gone.

You can consolidate. Drop the Amazon return at a UPS-authorized counter. Drop the Zappos return at a FedEx-authorized counter. Drop the Etsy return at the post office counter. Three stops. Three lines. Three different conversations with three different counter people.

Or you can go to Mailbox Plus.

## One Counter Does All Three

Mailbox Plus in Concord Township is an authorized drop-off for UPS, FedEx, and USPS. We also take DHL. One counter. One employee. One conversation.

You walk in with all three packages. We scan the Amazon UPS label. We scan the Zappos FedEx label. We scan the Etsy USPS label. Each goes into the correct carrier's bin. The carrier picks them up. You get the receipts in thirty seconds.

Fifteen minutes from Hambden. One stop. Not three.

## The Route

Take Girdled Road west to Route 44. Follow 44 south to Concord Township. Mailbox Plus is at 7554 Fredle Drive, behind the Sheetz. It is the same drive you would make to go to the UPS Store — but you leave all three boxes at one counter.

You are not saving money on gas. You are saving the second and third stops. You are saving the second and third conversations. You are saving the packing slip check-in process at three different counters.
