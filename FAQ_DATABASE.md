### USPS Services

**Q: What USPS services can I access at your location?**  
A: We are a full-service USPS authorized shipping center. We offer a wide range of services, including First-Class Mail®, Priority Mail®, Priority Mail Express®, USPS Ground Advantage™, international shipping, and more. We also sell postage stamps and provide mailing supplies.

**Q: Do you sell postage stamps?**  
A: Yes, we sell a variety of USPS postage stamps, including Forever® stamps and stamps for international mail, available as single stamps or in books.

**Q: Can I ship internationally using USPS from your store?**  
A: Absolutely. We can help you process international shipments using USPS services like Priority Mail International® and Priority Mail Express International®, including assistance with customs forms.

**Q: What do I need to know when preparing my package for USPS shipping?**  
A: Ensure your item is well-packed to prevent damage. If you use your own box, its dimensions may affect the price. For flat-rate boxes, ensure the contents fit without bulging. For any package over 10 oz or more than a half-inch thick with only stamps, you must bring it to the counter.

**Q: How is USPS postage calculated?**  
A: Postage is determined by the package's weight, dimensions, and the distance it's traveling. We can weigh your package and provide you with a price quote for various service options to find the best fit for your budget and timeline.

**Q: Are there items I am not allowed to ship with USPS?**  
A: Yes, USPS has restrictions on certain items. Prohibited materials include hazardous items like aerosols, firearms, flammable materials, liquids, and lithium batteries. If you're unsure about an item, please ask our staff for guidance.

**Q: What are the size and weight limits for USPS packages?**  
A: Generally, USPS packages cannot weigh more than 70 pounds. The combined length and girth (the distance around the thickest part) of the package typically cannot exceed 108 inches for most services.

**Q: Can I track my USPS shipment?**  
A: Yes, most USPS services include end-to-end tracking. We will provide you with a tracking number once your shipment is processed, which you can use on the USPS website to monitor your package's journey.

**Q: Do you offer special services like Certified Mail® or Signature Confirmation™?**  
A: Yes, we provide a range of extra services for your USPS shipments, including Certified Mail® for important documents, and Signature Confirmation™ to verify delivery. Just ask our staff, and we can add these to your shipment.

**Q: Can I drop off a pre-paid USPS package at your location?**  
A: Absolutely. If you have a package with a pre-paid USPS shipping label, you can drop it off with us for no additional charge. It's a quick and convenient way to get your package on its way.

**Q: When should I choose USPS over other carriers like FedEx or UPS?**  
A: USPS is often the most cost-effective option for smaller, lightweight packages (under 2-3 lbs) and for shipping to residential addresses, P.O. Boxes, and military bases. We can help you compare rates and delivery times to see which carrier best fits your specific needs.

## Specialty Services

### Digital Fingerprinting

**Q: Why is a fingerprint-based background check better than a name search?**  
A: Fingerprint-based checks are the most reliable and comprehensive because they match directly against state and federal criminal records. Unlike name-only searches, fingerprints cannot be falsified, reducing the hiring risk and ensuring more accurate results.

**Q: What is Live Scan fingerprinting and how does it work?**  
A: Live Scan is an electronic fingerprinting system that captures high-resolution digital prints in less than 5 minutes. Using SafePrintScan software and National WebCheck (NWC), FastFingerprints transmits your prints securely to state or federal agencies, avoiding errors and delays common with ink cards.

**Q: What is SafePrintScan?**  
A: SafePrintScan is FastFingerprints' proprietary software used with Live Scan systems. It ensures secure, encrypted fingerprint submissions to state and federal databases, provides storage and retrieval for digital records, and virtually eliminates processing errors.

**Q: Do you provide fingerprint cards?**  
A: Yes. For $35.00, fingerprints can be captured electronically and printed onto FD-258 fingerprint cards. This is useful for applications that require mailing fingerprint cards instead of electronic submission.

**Q: What are the costs for Ohio background checks?**  
A: Ohio background checks start at $40.00 for BCI-only. FBI-only checks are $52.00, and combined BCI & FBI checks are $72.00. Results are delivered through the secure Web Results portal.

**Q: What are the costs for Florida Level 2 background checks?**  
A: Florida Level 2 checks cost $75.00. With the AHCA-required photo included, the cost is $94.00. These checks search both state and FBI databases and are required for many licensing and employment positions.

**Q: What is an Ohio BCI check?**  
A: An Ohio BCI check is a state-level background check conducted by the Attorney General's Office. It is required for employment, volunteer work, or licensing in Ohio. Results are securely delivered through the Web Results portal.

**Q: What is a Florida Level 2 background check?**  
A: A Florida Level 2 check is a fingerprint-based search of FDLE state records and the FBI database. It is required for positions such as childcare, healthcare, and licensing in Florida. FastFingerprints is FDLE-approved to perform these checks.

**Q: What does it mean that FastFingerprints is an FBI-approved Channeler?**  
A: As an FBI Channeler, FastFingerprints can securely submit fingerprints to the FBI on behalf of authorized recipients. This service is used for expedited non-criminal justice purposes such as personal review, housing, or federally regulated industries.

**Q: Can I request my own FBI background check?**  
A: Yes. Individuals may request a copy of their FBI record for personal review or correction under Departmental Order 556-73. This cannot be used for employment or licensing in the U.S. Results must be sent to a U.S. address.

**Q: Are there restrictions on FBI Channeling?**  
A: Yes. FBI Channeling cannot be used for U.S. employment or licensing purposes. Results cannot be shipped overseas and an apostille cannot be processed by Channelers. Only U.S. citizens or lawful permanent residents are eligible.

**Q: Do you offer services besides fingerprinting?**  
A: Yes. FastFingerprints also provides drug screening and name search background checks in addition to electronic fingerprinting services.

**Q: Can fingerprints be stored and reused later?**  
A: Yes. Digital records can be securely stored, searched, and retrieved. This allows employers to capture prints during the interview process and only pay when a candidate is hired.

**Q: How long does the fingerprinting process take?**  
A: The actual fingerprint capture takes less than 5 minutes. Results are typically available quickly depending on the agency or recipient.

**Q: How secure is the fingerprint submission process?**  
A: All fingerprints are submitted electronically through encrypted channels using the National WebCheck system. This ensures secure, confidential, and accurate processing.

**Q: Do I need an appointment for fingerprinting?**  
A: Yes. Appointments must be made online at [register.fastfingerprints.com](https://register.fastfingerprints.com/account-entry). Walk-ins are not accepted.

**Q: Can I cancel or reschedule my appointment?**  
A: Yes. You can cancel or reschedule your fingerprinting appointment online at [fastfingerprints.com/cancel-appointment](https://www.fastfingerprints.com/cancel-appointment/).

**Q: How can I check my background check status or get my receipt?**  
A: You can check the status of your background check or retrieve your receipt online at [fastfingerprints.com/check-my-status](https://www.fastfingerprints.com/check-my-status/).

**Q: Can out-of-state applicants use your services?**  
A: Yes. FastFingerprints has a nationwide network of Live Scan locations, making it possible for applicants outside Ohio or Florida to complete fingerprinting services.

**Q: Where is Mailbox Plus located for fingerprinting services?**  
A: We are located in Gristmill Village, next to Pub Frato in Concord Township, Ohio. Our store provides convenient access to FastFingerprints services for Lake County residents, including Mentor, Painesville, and Eastlake.

### Insurance

**Q: What is shipping insurance?**  
A: Shipping insurance provides financial protection for your package if it is lost or damaged during transit. Our coverage is offered through Marsh, a trusted third-party insurer.

**Q: Why do I need shipping insurance?**  
A: Standard carrier liability is limited and may not fully cover the value of your items. Insurance ensures you're reimbursed up to the declared value if your package is lost or damaged.

**Q: How much coverage can I purchase?**  
A: We offer coverage up to $50,000 per package when packed by our professional staff, subject to Marsh's policy terms.

**Q: What types of items are eligible for insurance?**  
A: Most standard shipments can be insured. However, certain prohibited or restricted items—like cash, hazardous goods, or perishables—are not eligible. Ask us before shipping high-value or unusual items.

**Q: Do I need to use your packing services to qualify?**  
A: Yes. For insurance to apply, packages must be packed by our professional staff. This ensures your item is properly protected and meets claim requirements.

**Q: What proof of value do I need?**  
A: Claims require proof of value, such as a sales receipt, appraisal, or invoice. This ensures reimbursement matches the true value of your shipment.

**Q: How do I file a claim?**  
A: If your package is lost or damaged, notify us immediately. We'll guide you through the Marsh claim process and assist with the necessary documentation.

**Q: How long does it take to resolve a claim?**  
A: Most claims are processed within 2–4 weeks once all required documentation is provided, though timelines may vary by case.

**Q: Are there exclusions to coverage?**  
A: Yes. Coverage excludes prohibited items, improper packaging, concealed damage not reported promptly, and shipments not packed by our team.

**Q: How do I declare a shipment's value?**  
A: At the time of shipping, we'll record the declared value of your item. This amount determines the maximum coverage available.

**Q: Are fragile or antique items covered?**  
A: Yes, provided they are packed by our professional staff and declared at the correct value. Proper packing is critical for coverage eligibility.

**Q: Can oversized shipments be insured?**  
A: Yes, oversized items can be insured if they are professionally packed and fall within Marsh's coverage guidelines.

**Q: Does insurance cover international shipments?**  
A: Yes, insurance can cover international shipments, but some restrictions and additional documentation may apply depending on destination country.

**Q: How much does shipping insurance cost?**  
A: Rates are based on the declared value of your shipment. We'll calculate the cost at the time of shipping so you know exactly what you're paying for protection.

**Q: Can I insure multiple items in one shipment?**  
A: Yes, as long as the items are packed securely in one box or crate by our staff. Each package is insured separately by value.

**Q: How soon should I report damage?**  
A: Immediately. All visible damage must be reported at the time of delivery or as soon as possible to keep your claim valid.

**Q: Will insurance include tracking?**  
A: Insurance does not replace tracking but works alongside it. All insured shipments are shipped with a trackable service for added visibility.

**Q: What is the Peace of Mind Guarantee?**  
A: Our Peace of Mind Guarantee means your package is packed by professionals, insured up to its declared value, and handled with the highest level of care.
